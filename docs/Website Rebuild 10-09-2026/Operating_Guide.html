Cohort pipeline operating guide

Prepared by Team Alchemy for Alchemy, Sunil, client finance and the programme owner. 10 September 2026. This guide defines how the proposed backend will be used. No live database, messaging sequence or dashboard was activated in preparing it.

Start each working day with next actions

Alchemy checks new and unassigned leads, failed notifications, due tasks and replies. Assign every actionable lead to a named person, record the next action and date, and separate an enquiry from an application. A receipt means the system saved a request; it is not a personal reply or acceptance.

Read the experience and learning goal before responding. Clarify basic readiness and funding route, then coordinate Sunil’s technical-fit discussion. Record scheduled and held meetings separately. After a meeting, record the outcome and next action. Pause marketing automation before a manual conversation. Do not send a second conversation from another account without reviewing the existing history.

Sunil records technical fit, scope and final offers. Finance records receipts/refunds and references. The programme owner confirms the actual schedule with each member and records attendance separately. Alchemy never marks someone paid from an email click, positive reply or historical enrolment count.

The existing proposed service targets of one business day for acknowledgement and two for technical response remain subject to capacity agreement. Do not place them in public copy as guarantees. Use assigned task deadlines operationally; escalate overdue technical items to Sunil and finance questions to finance.

Weekly review

Review the same date window and cohort for acquisition, applications and operations. Reconcile unique applicants against saved records, payments against finance evidence, and attendance against the roster. Check missing source data, duplicate-contact candidates, unanswered questions and stage corrections. Record the action, owner and next review date.

Review objections qualitatively: fit, workload, funding, scope, timing and unanswered programme details. Label these from actual conversations, not inferred sentiment scores. Feed recurring questions into the page FAQs and future guides after Sunil validates the answers.

Marketing recipients are not applicants merely because they downloaded a resource. An employer sponsor is not a learner unless they separately apply. Enterprise organisations, orders and participants are different counting units. Keep the original four reported enrolments and two positive responses out of live totals until reconciled person by person.

Attribution and measurement boundaries

Keep first captured source, submission-session source and the self-reported discovery answer distinct. A person may discover Sunil on LinkedIn and apply later via a direct visit; retain that evidence without pretending to know unobserved sessions. Do not overwrite source with a guessed attribution model.

Backend application counts can exceed tracked successful sessions because analytics is blocked or declined. Show both with their definitions. A source chart for website sessions and a source chart for applications use different denominators. Do not claim one campaign caused a payment from a simple source tag.

Record post IDs in utm_content as already used by the campaign. Do not change current campaign links merely to distinguish a proposed rewrite; the original version remains active until selected. Once a rewrite goes live, record version and actual URL in a separate publication log.

Email operating procedure

Before the first live sequence, verify the approved sender, reply-to, email domain authentication, unsubscribe endpoint and reply ingestion. Use the exact reviewed template version. The sequence remains disabled if these dependencies fail.

Manual send: open lead history, confirm recipient and purpose, check consent and suppression, pause conflicting nurture, preview the rendered message, then send. Record the message and next action. A requested service reply does not require subscribing the recipient to marketing.

Nurture uses calendar-day offsets 2, 5 and 9 at 10:00 Asia/Kolkata. On a reply or meaningful stage change, stop the sequence and create a human follow-up task. On a manual conversation or on-hold stage, pause. Do not resume or restart merely because time has elapsed.

Incident handling: stop affected automated sends; preserve saved leads and provider IDs; reconcile unknown sends before retrying; notify the owner; document what happened and what was recovered. Do not export all prospect data into an incident message.

Assumptions and open inputs

Ein chooses the stack; no vendor fee or subscription is authorised by this guide. The initial release has no online payment collection. A data owner, actual sender, response capacity, retention policy and reply integration must be named before production. Sunil supplies the additional VSL recording and validates the spoken copy. The existing open programme/commercial inputs stay open until their owners resolve them.
