# LC-OCT-D01 The boundary your demo leaves open

An agent can recommend a refund without being allowed to issue it. The moment you connect the payment tool, the design question changes.

I would ask which evidence permits the action, how repeated requests are handled and who can intervene when the answer is uncertain. Those decisions belong in the architecture, before a convincing demo becomes a team commitment.

At The Living Craft, members build a working agentic system and practise explaining the choices behind it. The aim is to strengthen the ability to design, critique and guide a team’s architecture decisions.
