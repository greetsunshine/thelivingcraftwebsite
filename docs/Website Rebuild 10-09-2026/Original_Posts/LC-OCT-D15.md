# LC-OCT-D15 Feedback should tell you what to do next

Useful feedback names a strength, a gap and a next piece of work.

If a design handles a normal request but cannot explain a failed tool action, the follow-up should be specific. Add the missing behaviour, show the evidence and revisit the decision.

The Living Craft uses continuous feedback across the work. The programme does not offer a certificate. The focus is the capability you develop through building, evaluating and reviewing a system.
