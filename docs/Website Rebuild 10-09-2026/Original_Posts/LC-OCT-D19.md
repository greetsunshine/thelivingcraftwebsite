# LC-OCT-D19 One team or an industry case?

I would separate domain relevance from company-specific consulting before discussing a team programme.

Industry examples can make the learning relevant without requiring confidential company information. A company case needs a coherent team, a scoped problem and permission to use the material.

The group’s headcount alone cannot establish that scope. We need to understand the learning objective and what would count as useful work within the programme.

Request an enterprise scoping conversation before assuming a company problem is included.
