# LC-OCT-D23 When self-study may be enough

Self-study can be a good choice when you know what you need to learn and can evaluate your own work.

Live work becomes useful when you want another experienced perspective on the assumptions you are making. Can you explain a decision, hear a challenge and revise the design in response?

That is the experience I want to make concrete at The Living Craft: a working system, evidence and a useful architecture discussion. It should complement the reading and building you already do.

Explore the programme if that kind of feedback matches what you need next.
