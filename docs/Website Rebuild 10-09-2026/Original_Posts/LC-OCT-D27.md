## Spoken script

When a team brings me a design, I first want to understand the decision it is trying to make.

What task should the system perform? Which constraints matter? Where can it act, and where should it stop? Then I ask the team to explain the evidence behind its choices.

The review becomes useful when we can locate an uncertainty and agree the next piece of work. Perhaps we need to inspect a failed tool action, narrow a permission or compare two approaches against the same task.

Guiding the team means helping it make that decision clearly. It does not require replacing every choice with my preferred design.
