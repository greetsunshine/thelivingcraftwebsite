# LC-OCT-D01 The boundary your demo leaves open

Imagine a returns assistant giving a perfectly sensible answer: the customer should get a refund.

Everyone can follow the reasoning. Then someone connects the payment tool.

Now the assistant can move money. That small addition deserves a much bigger conversation.

Who allowed the refund? What evidence was enough? If the customer asks twice, do we pay twice? When the answer is uncertain, who gets the next decision?

A good demo makes the happy path easy to see. I want the design to make these less comfortable questions visible too.

That is the connection we practise at The Living Craft: build a working agentic system, examine what it does, and explain the choices behind it.
