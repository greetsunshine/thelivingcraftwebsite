# LC-OCT-D05 Fit is about responsibility

A job title tells me very little about the design conversation we could have.

A trade-off tells me much more.

For example, imagine describing a system that became easier to operate after the team gave up a feature. What made that choice sensible? What did it cost? Would you make it again?

Now we have something useful to discuss.

That is the starting point I care about for The Living Craft. Prior system-design exposure, a question worth working on, and willingness to build and revise through feedback.

You can work with an industry case. You do not need to bring a confidential company project.

If that sounds like your next step, describe your experience and learning goal in the open-cohort application.
