# LC-OCT-D13 A cheaper response can cost more

Imagine celebrating a smaller model bill, then asking three people to fix what the workflow got wrong.

The saving looked better before the rest of the cost arrived.

That is why I would look beyond the price of one response. Retries, review time, delay and the consequence of an incorrect action all belong in the conversation.

A more capable model is not automatically the answer either. A bounded task may work well with a simpler approach.

First define the task. Then compare the behaviour and the full cost of getting the job done. Otherwise, we can optimise the easiest number to see and miss the work happening around it.
