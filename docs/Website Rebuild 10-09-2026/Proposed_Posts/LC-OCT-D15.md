# LC-OCT-D15 Feedback should tell you what to do next

“Needs improvement” can keep someone busy for a very long time. It does not tell them where to start.

Imagine a design that handles the normal request well but loses track of what happened when a tool fails.

Now the feedback can be useful: keep the part that works, make the uncertain result visible, decide who handles it, and come back with the revised behaviour.

The next conversation has something new to examine.

That is what I want feedback to do at The Living Craft. Help you see a strength, locate a gap and work on a concrete next step. We keep returning to the working system and the reasoning behind it.

The programme does not offer a certificate. Explore the open cohort if this kind of practice and feedback is what you need next.
