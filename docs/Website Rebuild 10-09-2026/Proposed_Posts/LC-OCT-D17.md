# LC-OCT-D17 Give a manager a decision they can make

Put yourself in the manager’s chair for a moment.

“Can you fund this course?” is a request for money. It leaves quite a lot of work for the person reading it.

Which responsibility will it help with? What do you want to practise? How much time will you need? Where could you use the learning?

I would answer those questions in the request itself. For example: you review agent designs, and you want to strengthen how you judge the evidence behind a team’s choices.

The Living Craft includes practical building, continuous feedback and 30 live hours plus independent work. Confirm the schedule and independent-work expectation before asking for final funding approval.

The manager summary puts the individual sponsorship route into one conversation. Use it to discuss employer funding for your open-cohort application.
