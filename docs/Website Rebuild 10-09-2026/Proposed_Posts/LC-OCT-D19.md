# LC-OCT-D19 One team or an industry case?

Imagine a team arriving at a learning programme with the sentence: “We have a company problem we could use.”

That could mean a useful example. It could also mean a production project with three other teams waiting for it.

We need to know which conversation we are having.

An industry case can make practice relevant without bringing confidential company material into the room. A company-specific case needs a coherent team, a clear problem and permission to use the information.

The headcount cannot answer those questions for us. The learning objective and agreed scope can.

Before assuming your company problem is included, request an enterprise scoping conversation. We can work out which case approach fits the team.
