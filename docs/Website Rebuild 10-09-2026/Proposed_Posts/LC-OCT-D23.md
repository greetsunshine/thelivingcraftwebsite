# LC-OCT-D23 When self-study may be enough

Sometimes the best next step is to close the course tab and build the thing.

If you know what you need to learn and can judge your own work, self-study may give you exactly what you need.

The harder moment is when the system works and you are still unsure whether the design makes sense.

Imagine explaining it to another engineer who asks one question you had not considered. Now you have a new assumption to examine, or a reason to change your approach.

That is where live practice and feedback can help.

At The Living Craft, I want the working system to give us something concrete to discuss: the choices, the evidence and what to try next. It should build on the reading and experimenting you already do.

Explore the programme if that is the kind of feedback you are looking for.
