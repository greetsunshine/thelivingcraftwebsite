# LC-OCT-D25 A useful application explains the starting point

“I want to learn AI” is a starting point. It leaves us with a few questions.

“Here is the kind of system I work on, and here is a design decision I want to get better at” gives us a conversation.

That is what a useful application should do. Help us understand your starting point.

Tell us about your system-design experience, the responsibility you hold and one question you would like to work on. Let us know whether you are self-funded or need employer approval.

There is no need to send confidential company architecture. An explanation in your own words is enough to begin.

We can then discuss fit, schedule and terms before you commit. Apply to explore your place in The Living Craft’s open cohort.
