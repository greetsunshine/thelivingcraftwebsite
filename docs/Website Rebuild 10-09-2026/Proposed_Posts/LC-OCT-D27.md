## Spoken script

Imagine finishing a design review with a document full of comments. Everyone contributed. Then someone asks, “So, what do we do next?”

If we cannot answer that, we still have work to do.

I would start by asking which decision the team is trying to make. What task should the system perform? What constraints matter? Where can it act, and where should it stop?

Then we can look at the evidence behind the proposed approach. Perhaps a tool failure needs closer inspection. Perhaps a permission is too broad. Perhaps we should compare two approaches on the same task.

The next step should be concrete enough for the team to take and bring back. That is more useful than replacing every choice with the reviewer’s favourite design.
