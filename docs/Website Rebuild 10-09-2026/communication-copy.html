Subject: What helps shape a useful team programme

A useful scoping conversation covers participant experience, the engineering sponsor, industry context and scheduling constraints.

Enterprise programmes normally involve 8–10 participants. Industry-specific examples and assignments are the default. A company case needs a coherent team, agreed boundaries and permission to use the material.

Reply with the context you can share, or let us know who should join the discussion. Final scope and commercial terms are agreed with Sunil.
