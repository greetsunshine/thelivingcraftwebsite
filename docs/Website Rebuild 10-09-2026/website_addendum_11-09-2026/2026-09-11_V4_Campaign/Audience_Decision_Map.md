# Audience decision map

LC-STRATEGY-V4.0.0. Concerns below are hypotheses until supported by actual questions. They determine the job of the content, not a psychological label for an individual.

| Reader decision | Concern hypothesis | Evidence/content | Route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Is this relevant to my work? | Tools are moving faster than my design confidence | Recognisable use cases, boundaries, cost and release decisions | Standalone Sunil stories and practical company reviews |
| Can Sunil help me reason? | A practitioner may know more than they can explain | Worked review that starts with behaviour, asks a question and shows a testable revision | D04/D06/D08/D14 |
| Why learn together rather than use a resource? | Free material might be enough | Explain a working build, continuous feedback, reasoning and revision without disparaging independent study | D12/D22 and cohort page |
| Am I ready? | I may be too inexperienced or need a company project | Prior system-design exposure, learning goal, industry case and confidentiality boundary | D18/D30 |
| Can I make the commitment? | Time, workload and funding are unclear | Confirmed programme facts, final-offer process and employer discussion | D24/D26 |
| How do I take the next step? | Applying might commit me before I have answers | Clear application/enquiry choices and distinct fit, offer and payment steps | D30/D32 |
| Is this for one person or my team? | Individual sponsorship and enterprise learning are mixed together | Separate person-level membership from organisation scoping | D28 and enquiry routing |

Ask early prospects what responsibility they want to develop, which explanation was useful and what they still need to decide. Record volunteered answers in the lead history. Five optional qualitative conversations can improve execution; they are not a condition for locking strategy or evidence of conversion rates.
