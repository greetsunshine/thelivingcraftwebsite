# V4 demand and member handling

LC-STRATEGY-V4.0.0. Objective: eight qualified members attending; no forecast conversion rates.

Content and relevant approved conversations can lead directly to the cohort page. Resources are optional. An anonymous resource visit is not a lead. An identified resource request, enquiry and application are distinct activities attached to the same person where appropriate.

Alchemy handles new enquiries, basic experience/goal/funding clarification, routing and meeting coordination. Capture the actual question without inferring emotions or technical fit. A requested response must be relevant to that request; downloading alone is not permission for unsolicited nurture.

Sunil evaluates technical fit and owns scope and offers. Finance confirms invoice/payment/refund evidence. Programme ownership confirms readiness and roster, then actual attendance. Keep scheduled and held meetings distinct. Employer-funded individuals remain individual members. Enterprise enquiries create organisation-level scoping with distinct participant records.

Review unassigned leads, overdue tasks, replies and delivery failures daily. Record the next action and owner. Response-time commitments require capacity confirmation; do not promise an SLA from this guide. Stop or pause approved nurture on the retained reply/stage/permission conditions.

For a cohort enquiry, establish the question and offer the relevant programme explanation or fit conversation. For an application, acknowledge the saved application and coordinate the next step. For an enterprise enquiry, establish participant readiness, sponsor, learning objective and procurement route before Sunil scopes it. These are handling guidelines, not messages sent or final-offer authority.

Use the retained 10 September operating guide for detailed staff workflows and the V4 reporting controls for measurement. Do not change historical person/organisation IDs when importing trackers.
