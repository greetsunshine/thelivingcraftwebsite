# The Living Craft — V4 campaign strategy

LC-STRATEGY-V4.0.0. Selected by the user's implementation instruction on 11 September 2026, ahead of the 12 September lock deadline. This fixes strategic direction; exact content, recordings and publication have separate review states.

## The decision

Design agentic systems. Guide your team.

The campaign helps experienced engineers and technical leads recognise a relevant design problem, trust Sunil's judgment, experience his teaching and decide whether to apply. The objective is eight qualified open-cohort members attending. This is an objective, not a forecast or an eight-seat availability claim.

Use 32 posts, alternating from Sunil: 16 on each account. Sunil has six written posts, five carousels and five videos. The company has eight carousels and eight statics. Total: five videos, 13 carousels, eight statics and six written posts. The website VSL is additional.

Sunil's 16 product-development topics provide the spine. Each post begins with a decision or recognisable situation, develops the reasoning and ends with an appropriate next step. Every post works on its own. A reader should not need earlier parts or knowledge of the household product to understand its relevance.

## Why this campaign can support joining

Experience establishes credibility; teaching demonstrates how Sunil helps someone reason; programme explanations help a person decide. None of these alone is sufficient. The company posts therefore support use-case reviews, worked comparisons, resources, prerequisites, workload, employer sponsorship and applications alongside the stories.

Audience concerns are hypotheses: uncertainty beyond the demo, scepticism about another course, difficulty defending design choices, limited time and employer-funding questions. Use real enquiries and conversations to refine language. Do not infer an individual's feelings from browsing or claim that these hypotheses are research findings.

Member and employer-sponsored individual are one cohort route. Enterprise buyers need a separate organisation-level scoping route. A director's interest is not automatically a team purchase. This is not a startup-building course or a promise to deliver a production implementation for an employer.

## Voice and evidence

Sunil is an experienced engineer, active builder and mentor who explains difficult decisions clearly. Personal stories require source evidence and publication permission. The PDF is a supplied account of experience, not independent verification. The current copy uses complete illustrative examples and conditional first-person reasoning; it makes no unsupported claim that an incident happened to Sunil.

Record which source page supports an eventual story, the date and system version, the measured quantity, what happened afterwards and who cleared the disclosure. Keep product-confidential details and personal data out. A redrawn diagram still needs a disclosure review.

Avoid generic praise, emojis, invented anecdotes and absolute engineering rules. Teach a useful small solution rather than withholding the answer to manufacture a reason to buy. Explain where it applies and what remains uncertain. A second model does not establish independent judgment merely by being from another family; passing tests does not certify all future behaviour.

## The path to an application

Sunil's topics 4, 8, 12 and 16 invite exploration/application directly. Other Sunil posts invite a practical action or discussion. The company introduces the programme in its first post and continues to answer joining questions. Links must lead to a tested, matching destination.

Post or relevant conversation → useful explanation or resource → cohort page → application or enquiry → saved record and acknowledgement → Alchemy basic qualification → Sunil technical fit → approved offer → finance-confirmed payment → attendance confirmation → actual attendance.

Resources are not compulsory steps before applying. Resource requests are not applications and do not establish marketing permission. Use open HTML and optional download/email where approved; give people a direct path to the cohort and an enquiry route.

## Production and freeze

The topic map, audience, positioning, account roles, format mix and conversion journey are selected. Factual corrections and changes to individual executions can continue without reopening them. A genuine strategic change needs a dated decision explaining why it changes the enrolment objective; preference for a different hook is not one.

Three samples establish delivery: D01 written, D05 carousel, D03 video. All 32 posts have complete copy. Produce in four batches of eight. Batch 1 has complete copy, 25 editable panel drafts and recording scripts for D03/D07. Five social recordings remain Sunil's responsibility; Alchemy finishes video edits after footage arrives. No recording date is invented.

Release cost-ceiling worksheet LC-R01 before D10, evaluation-gates worksheet LC-R02 before D16 and deployment checklist LC-R03 before D20. The other 13 artifacts remain a backlog. Do not make a full toolkit a launch dependency.

The content calendar begins on the actual first approved publication date. It is not a countdown to an unconfirmed cohort start. If a required route, resource or recording is unavailable, keep that asset unscheduled and escalate its owner; do not silently publish an unavailable promise or overwrite the selected format. A dated tactical schedule decision may release an independently approved later asset while keeping its ID.

## Commercial and release boundaries

Use 30 live hours plus independent work. The amount and interval of independent work, confirmed session schedule, final prices, taxes, refund/access/catch-up arrangements and eligibility remain unresolved. Six weeks and October planning references are not a confirmed schedule. Do not publish the PDF's price simply because a code file contained it.

Alchemy owns approved initial handling, qualification and follow-up coordination. Sunil owns technical fit, programme scope and final offers. Finance owns invoices, receipts and refunds. Programme ownership controls attendance. Ein implements and maintains the website/backend from the functional handoff.

The local V4 package is not public release approval, a live backend test, an email activation or a change to issued commercial documents. Organic content and approved relevant outreach work together; optional paid activity requires its own bounded approval.

## Review what leads towards attendance

Review execution weekly: posts published, relevant questions, identifiable resource interest, qualified applications, conversations held, offers, finance-confirmed paid members and attendance. Diagnose the step that is not moving before rewriting the strategy. Do not treat impressions, downloads, followers or a booked meeting as a paid member.

The reporting baseline is unknown. No conversion-rate forecast is supplied. Content may build trust, but timely responses, technical-fit conversations, confirmed terms and a working application route also affect enrolment.
