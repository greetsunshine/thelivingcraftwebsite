## How the writing should sound

Use a recognisable decision, explain the consequence and leave the reader with a useful question or method. Vary the openings; do not repeat a formula in every caption. Keep first-person singular where Sunil explains his approach, including company posts authored in his voice. Avoid invented memories and claims of having observed all teams or most systems.

Until an incident is verified, use an explicitly illustrative situation and “I would” reasoning. After clearance, replace that section with the dated actual incident and its supported consequences; keep the reader's decision and the post's assigned purpose intact. A copy change invalidates the prior exact-asset approval.

A model-family choice, deterministic scorer, confidence band or deployment pattern is a decision under conditions, not a universal rule. Explain limits. Useful teaching can reveal the control; paid learning adds application, feedback, depth and guided revision.
